# Aviation Ground School Academy — instructor review matrix

**Status: DRAFT / NO SIGNED APPROVAL** · 13 November 2026

The Academy's 12 subject modules contain 72 educational sections, 72 self-check questions and 12 applied case questions. This matrix is an **editorial review aid**, not a regulator-accepted syllabus mapping, EASA-approved ATO material, Sierra Leone CAA authorisation, or a substitute for properly licensed instructors.

## Source anchors and editorial subject categories

- FAA [Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak), FAA-H-8083-25C. Chapter names below are broad suggested reading areas; verify exact sections in the current source and any addenda.
- FAA [Airplane Flying Handbook](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook) for practical context; not a source of generic real-world operating limits in this Academy.
- EASA [Easy Access Rules for Aircrew](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-aircrew). Subject categories are used only to organise future editorial checking; actual PPL(A), PPL(H) or other training requirements must be checked directly.
- The Sierra Leone CAA and official AIP/NOTAM channels must be consulted to validate local legal and procedural content; no claims of current local regulatory compliance are made here.

## Required checks before an authorised release

1. An identified, appropriately qualified instructor verifies each learning objective, correct answer, distractor and practical worked example. Check exact statements against the **current source edition**, not a webpage title.
2. A responsible academic reviewer records the applicable jurisdiction, licence category, training standard, classroom age range, prerequisites and desired learning depth. Air Scout activities require youth safeguarding and an independent approval process.
3. A safety reviewer checks all performance, weather, airspace, radio and rotorcraft passages for operational ambiguity, potentially dangerous inference or outdated limitations.
4. A course author corrects all findings, versions the affected questions and lessons, and signs an audit record. A new practice-bank revision should not silently count old scores as current.
5. The institution must establish secure student registration, consent where appropriate, payments, instructor identity, assessment protection, retrievable student records and documented certificate issuance criteria before describing any output as formal training credit.

### Proposed sign-off fields (not implemented as authentication)

Lesson revision, qualified reviewer identity, role/organisation, credentials verified by, relevant regulatory reference and edition, evidence page/section, editorial findings, corrections made, retest results, approval date and authorisation signature.
