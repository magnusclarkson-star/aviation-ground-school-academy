# Course 09 — Radiotelephony and ATC Awareness: picture-led radio practice

**Prepared:** 14 November 2026 · **Module:** `communications` (Course **09**) · **Academic status:** `SME_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

## Delivery

A compact, four-part radio practical now appears within Course 09 **Study Activity** of the existing 12-module Academy Coursebook. Existing introductory reading, deep lessons, original teaching images, standard course quizzes, order 01–12, student result records, simulator controls and accepted aircraft/certificate designs are unchanged.

The practical reuses only **source-preserving crops** of the original, owner-approved Academy radio poster illustrations `overview-06` and `detail-07`. Six image views are provided; each can be tapped to enlarge. It introduces no replacement panels or cockpit models.

| Exercise | New learner interaction |
|---|---|
| First contact | Compare three fictional first-contact calls; identify called station, speaker, position/context and purpose |
| Spelling | Tap any of **26** international radiotelephony alphabet letters or type a short fictional set of letters for in-app spelling; correct **Alfa** and **Juliett** spellings |
| Hold-short readback correction | Compare an invented instruction to hold short Runway **09**, a deliberately wrong Runway **27** readback and the corrected sequence |
| Communication uncertainty | Distinguish an unreadable message from proof of radio failure or an actual clearance; seek explanation using qualified instruction |

All four contain original illustrative pictures, separate Air Scout/aspiring-pilot learning cues, three multiple-choice answers, corrective feedback and five-item expandable terminology keys.

### Fictional pilot–controller dialogues

Each exercise includes a **scripted fictional dialogue** with a user-initiated **Listen to example**, **Stop audio** and **Show/Hide transcript** action. Supported browsers may synthesize speech using locally available browser/device voices; controller and pilot/students use slightly different voice pitch and rate. Playback quality and availability depend on the device and browser. There are **no actual ATC audio recordings**, authentic callsigns, operating frequencies or live radio connection. Audio does not start automatically. When synthesized speech is unavailable, the transcript remains fully readable.

The exercise does **not** request microphone access, record learners, upload sound, create downloadable notes/audio files or persist answers. The spelling input accepts only letters/spaces and is restricted to 24 characters, displayed in current-page memory. Do not enter real personal identifiers.

### Educational accuracy and operational boundaries

The international spelling alphabet includes **Alfa** (not “Alpha”) and **Juliett** (not “Juliet”), as specified in ICAO Annex 10 Volume II. The illustrated **hold-short** exchange concerns an invented `Training Seven Two` student and station called `Training Ground`; it is **not** a real ATC clearance or a Sierra Leone airport operating procedure. Actual readback requirements depend on applicable law and aviation communications procedures.

Illustrative training line (not an operational instruction):

> Controller: “Training Seven Two, hold short runway zero niner.”  
> Student, deliberately wrong: “Holding short runway two seven, Training Seven Two.”  
> Controller: “Correction, hold short runway zero niner.”  
> Learner: “Holding short runway zero niner, Training Seven Two.”

A holding instruction is distinct from permission to enter, cross or take off from any real runway. The Academy does not teach an actual communication-failure or emergency checklist here; qualified instructors and current type-/jurisdiction-specific sources are required.

**Reviewer source references** (not authority to apply US procedures in Sierra Leone):
- ICAO, history of Annex 10's international radiotelephony spelling alphabet: https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/postalhistory/annex_10_aeronautical_telecommunications.htm
- FAA, Air Traffic Control, §2-4-3 Pilot Acknowledgment/Read Back: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap2_section_4.html
- Current Sierra Leone Civil Aviation Authority rules, applicable AIP/communications procedures, authorised service providers and ICAO Annex 10 Volume II must be reviewed by a qualified instructor **before** use as formal local training.
