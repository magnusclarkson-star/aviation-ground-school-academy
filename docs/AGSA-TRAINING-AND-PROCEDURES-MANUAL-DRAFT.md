## 1. Purpose, boundaries and control

This manual is an **initial organisational template**, not evidence of an ATO certificate, approved course, qualified staff or any right to issue aircraft licences. When approved, it should define who trains, what is authorised, how training is conducted and recorded, and how the Academy ensures ongoing conformity to its actual approved training specifications.

Approved training types/ratings and certificate reference: **NONE — application to be discussed with SLCAA**. The public Academy provides only introductory self-study. Its browser simulator is neither an approved flight simulation training device nor an acceptable substitute for logged flying experience.

Changes to syllabus, key personnel, location, facilities, courses or approved training equipment must be assessed against Part 1B §§2.3.8 and 2.4 before use. The Head of Training owns version control, the Quality Manager checks compliance and the Accountable Manager confirms organisational resources. Where required, submit amendments to SLCAA and wait for authorisation before using them for approved training. Keep controlled distribution and obsolete-version records.

## 3. Course admission, progression and credit

For each proposed approved course, a signed syllabus must specify:
- The relevant licence/rating or defined standalone training authorisation and the applicable jurisdiction.
- Entry prerequisites, medical/experience/age standards where relevant.
- Measurable learning objectives, flight/ground split, required hours and structured lesson-by-lesson time.
- Classroom, equipment, approved aircraft and aerodromes, and any qualified FSTD and its authorised credit.
- Instruction method, exact assessment blueprint, standards to progress between stages, remediation, retakes, appeals and completion criteria.

**Current Academy coursebook:** 12 public introductory subject modules, 72 reading sections and practice checks. The material is not verified, not of approved duration and is not proof of competency; it must be rewritten or extended according to an agreed syllabus.

A candidate must not move from private browser quiz results to formal records automatically. A head-of-training-approved enrolment, eligible instructor, verified evidence and actual performance are required. The private backend stores staff-recorded stages, exam references and independent quality attestation only under verified external-approval gates.

## 4. Training conduct and instructor standardisation

Before each teaching activity, the responsible instructor must verify the syllabus revision, venue/system readiness, training materials and student prerequisites. Record attendance, objective, duration, delivery method, instructor identity, student performance, stage result and follow-up where relevant. Practical and flight teaching requires separate aircraft-specific manuals and qualified supervision; nothing in this template is an operating checklist.

Conduct periodic instructor standardisation to align terminology, marking rubrics, student feedback, accessibility and threat/error-management content. Document any issues and corrective actions. Respect the Part 1B §2.5 duty limitations and any approved instructor workload policies.

## 5. Knowledge checks and formal examining

The public self-study questions are open and cannot be used as controlled licensing examinations. A future authority-accepted assessment programme must maintain a private versioned item bank, identity verification, approved assessment objectives, correct standard-setting, re-examination policy, controlled access, records of examiner privileges and procedures for appeal or suspected misconduct.

Only an appropriately authorised examiner under specific examining authority may create an official assessment record; the private backend requires independent documentary evidence. The assessor must attach a protected, auditable external evidence record and not rely on client-supplied scores or browser localStorage. The system does **not** proctor online exams and does **not** issue pilot licences. Stage exam standards and examiner rights are **TBD**.

## 6. Training and staff records

Records include identification/eligibility, approved course revision, attendance, stage assessments, instructor records, exam report reference, remedial outcomes, graduation decision and all relevant amendments. Retain records at least as prescribed by Part 1B §2.3.12, subject to longer legal obligations, access controls and an appropriate records-retention schedule. No regulated training records should be placed on the public GitHub Pages site.

The private records service has preliminary role-gated append-only exam/stage records and audit events. It is not production accepted without backup restore tests, encryption and privacy review, breach-response procedures, identity provider/MFA, audit independence and regulator acceptance.

## 7. Quality assurance and safety management

The Quality Manager establishes a risk-based audit schedule, samples course delivery against each syllabus, logs nonconformities, assigns actions, verifies closure and reports issues to the Accountable Manager. Audit independence and a documented corrective-action process are required.

When applicable, the Safety Manager establishes confidential hazard reporting, risk assessment, mitigation, occurrence escalation and quality/SMS interfaces per Part 19. Student safeguarding, safe supervision and privacy of minors are additional organisational prerequisites. Do not enable connected youth accounts before a documented safeguarding process.

## 8. Facilities and equipment

Approved training requires facilities, working conditions, instruction aids, accessibility and applicable equipment acceptable to the Authority. Any training aircraft and aerodromes must be included in the approved course specifications, with appropriate maintenance and inspection. The Academy's simulated Cessna and R44 are educational visuals only, not approved synthetic devices for training credit.

## 9. Complaints, academic integrity and appeals

Provide students with an accessible private channel to challenge records, inappropriate instruction and decisions without retaliation. Separate the examiner's original mark from the independent appeal review. Preserve relevant evidence and audit history, correct inaccurate records through documented append-only amendments and avoid overwriting historical outcomes.

## 10. Management review and manuals register

At intervals agreed in the approved quality system, review instructor currency, student results, complaints, exam standards, security issues, audit findings, safety reports and readiness of facilities/equipment. Log amendments, review status, personnel trained on new revisions and Authority acceptance where required.
