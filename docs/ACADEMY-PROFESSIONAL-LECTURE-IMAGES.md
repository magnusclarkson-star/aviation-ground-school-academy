# Ground School professional pictorial references

**Edition:** visual editorial upgrade, 13 November 2026.

The Academy's 12-module Ground School coursebook now pairs **17 externally hosted reference images** with **12 original locally rendered and labelled SVG concept diagrams**. Images are used when they help make the lesson more concrete: aircraft anatomy, real flight instruments, electrical connections, actual thunderstorm forms, real charts and operational research. Existing quiz results, student progress, aircraft models and flight simulator source remain untouched.

Each externally sourced item is accompanied, in the **actual coursebook UI**, by an author/agency credit, a licence link and a link to the exact Wikimedia Commons source page. The displayed source file is not modified or rebranded. The following editorial references are also captured in `academy-coursebook-professional-media.js`:

References are available under their individual source and licence terms. Third-party imagery is retrieved at runtime from Wikimedia Commons and may not be available offline or if the image host is blocked. The **12 original SVG diagrams always remain locally packaged** in the Academy website as a teaching fallback; the photo renderer also shows a message if both Wikimedia thumbnail and original URLs fail. No learner data is transmitted with the photo requests; the image requests are independent of private student systems.

## Technical and teaching limitations

- The FAA aircraft electrical-system diagram is a published reference example, **not** an authorised wiring diagram for a specific aircraft.
- The wind-tunnel photograph represents a specific negative angle of attack, not normal flight at every airspeed.
- The historic Kodiak chart is **not current navigation material** and depicts a US area, not Sierra Leone.
- The US airspace illustration is **not** Sierra Leone's airspace structure; current State AIP and charts apply.
- A cockpit photograph is a reference and not an exact simulator instrument calibration.
- A real rotorcraft photo identifies aircraft features; it does not replace type-specific pilot instruction.
- All diagrams and captions remain educational drafts pending qualified aviation instructor review.

**Technical arrangement:** primary reference photo in the appropriate introductory lesson; where a second image is selected, it appears in the advanced lessons rather than duplicating the introductory picture. On touch or keyboard, a photo opens an accessible large-size viewer. Printed notes preserve the photograph, caption and attribution. Self-contained original SVG concepts can be enlarged separately.
