**Subject:** Request for an ATO pre-application meeting — Aviation Ground School Academy (online theoretical training)

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request pre-application guidance regarding the proposed **Aviation Ground School Academy**, an independently operated aviation education initiative being developed in Sierra Leone.

At present the Academy offers introductory online theoretical lessons and an educational visual simulator. It **does not** claim to be an approved training organisation, an authorised flight simulation training device, or a provider of licensing examination credits.

Our intention is to explore what organisational approval and course-specific authorisations would be required to offer **formally approved ground-theory instruction**, and whether a limited initial syllabus or a supervised partnership with an existing certificated ATO would be an appropriate first step under Sierra Leone's current Civil Aviation Regulations.

We would be grateful for confirmation of:

1. The applicable SLCAR Part 1B scope and current ATO application requirements, including required physical facilities and eligible delivery methods for online theoretical instruction.
2. The current prospective ATO pre-assessment statement (PATOPS/O-PEL-026), formal ATO application forms, training specifications templates, fees, lead times and the pre-application meeting process.
3. The qualifications and positions required for our proposed Accountable Manager, Head of Training, Quality Manager, ground instructors and authorised examiners, according to the courses to be offered.
4. The process for SLCAA review of a draft theoretical syllabus, stage standards, assessment strategy, student records and the Training and Procedures Manual.
5. The separate requirements for approved examining authority and for any future qualification of a flight simulation training device; no simulator training credit is currently claimed.
6. Any current guidance on safeguarding, digital student recordkeeping, remote teaching and training certification/verification mechanisms applicable to a proposed online Academy.
