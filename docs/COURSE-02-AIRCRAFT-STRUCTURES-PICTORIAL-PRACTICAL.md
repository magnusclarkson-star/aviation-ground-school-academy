# Course 02 — Aircraft Structures & Configurations: pictorial practical
**Revision:** 14 November 2026 · **Status:** classroom self-study enhancement, qualified instructor review pending

## Existing approved material preserved

This addition sits beneath the original **Course 02 Study Activity** in `academy-coursebook.html?module=aircraft`. The owner's previously approved full Cessna teaching plate, aircraft models, R44 exterior/interior, fighter models, cockpit layouts and every existing coursebook lesson remain unchanged.

The new lab references only source-pixel *views* of existing owner-approved teaching images in the Academy picture atlas. It never saves an edited image over its original.

## Four new exercises

| Exercise | Picture(s) already approved | Student task |
|---|---|---|
| Main airframe assemblies | `cessna-01` | Distinguish wing, fuselage, empennage and landing gear from cockpit instruments/rotors |
| Conventional control surfaces | `cessna-02` | Match ailerons to roll, elevator to pitch, rudder to yaw; distinguish flaps |
| Three flight axes | `cessna-03` | Identify longitudinal/roll, lateral/pitch and vertical/yaw axes |
| Aeroplane vs helicopter | `cessna-01` + `overview-10` | Compare fixed lifting wing with powered rotor and helicopter controls |

Each exercise shows the relevant original cropped picture (tap to enlarge), a scenario, three responses, feedback and an expandable part-and-function key. Scouts and enthusiasts/pilots see different explanation prompts. Progress through these four activities is **only in the current page memory**, with no upload, verified grade or certificate entitlement. Existing Academy subject checks remain separate.

Content is conceptual, not an aircraft-specific maintenance manual, flight checklist or signed aviation training credit.

## In-app-only lesson materials

The Academy now suppresses **all browser print and Save as PDF output of lesson notes, diagrams and picture pages** for the following eight learning interfaces: the Ground School Coursebook, two specialist course pages, the Academy original picture gallery, AI Instructor Studio, four Ground-School Modules, Study Lab, and Aircraft Learning Paths. Instead, print media shows an informational message redirecting students to study inside the Academy.

The **Print these teaching notes** command has been removed from every module's full-textbook panel. There is no one-click download of coursebook lessons. Separate *practice-score exports* still contain only editable self-check results, not lesson content. Authorised certificates and transcripts remain distinct document workflows; do not remove their official downloads when imposing study-note restrictions.

### Technical limitation

This is a **browser-UI restriction**, not DRM or copy protection: a person can still take screenshots, copy visible text or download publicly served JS/image assets. The Academy's current public GitHub Pages material is statically hosted. Making course content genuinely access-controlled requires moving private course content to a server with authenticated enrolments, administrator-controlled access, secure licensing and tested privacy/safeguarding rules, rather than simply blocking printing.
