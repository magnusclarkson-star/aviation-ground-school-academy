## What students study

The coursebook's existing Course 08 teaching, original approved pictures, extended written chapters, practice questions and corrected Course 1–12 navigation are preserved. The new picture-led practical is an **additive** section beneath the Course 08 Study Activity; it contains four ungraded exercises.

| Exercise | Original owner-approved pictures | New interactive educational material |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal and vertical airspace limits | `detail-12` (flight-planning panel), `overview-05` (original chart artwork) | Diagram showing a wholly fictional airspace volume; learner selects lateral boundary, floor, ceiling and effective-status descriptions |
| Aerodrome surfaces and holding position | `overview-05`, `photo-02` (training aircraft on apron) | Compact **invented airport diagram** with runway 09/27, Taxiway A, apron and hold position; tap labels for their meaning |
| Runway designations | `overview-05` | Select fictitious runway 09, 18, 27 or 36 and compare reciprocal designations, their approximate magnetic directions and limitations |
| State responsibility and authoritative information | `detail-12` | Distinguish Academy learning from binding applicable State law, current AIP, official NOTAMs and any specific operating authorisation |

Students review a short fictional situation, answer three choices, receive corrective feedback, and can open a concise five-term concept key. Original artwork is shown through source-preserving canvas crops; it is neither replaced nor edited. Air Scouts have adult-supervised prompts, and enthusiasts/pilots have regulatory-source prompts.

**These are fictional layouts, not actual Sierra Leone airspace or aerodrome charts.** The interactive boundaries, runway, hold line, labels and airspace volume are educational aids only. Their colours, proportions, dimensions and paths do **not** establish a current aerodrome sign, a real published boundary, live runway condition, obstacle clearance or operational permission.

## Sierra Leone regulatory grounding and review requirements

The **Sierra Leone Civil Aviation Authority (SLCAA)** is the national regulatory source context. Official Sierra Leone Civil Aviation Regulations **Part 15A (Aeronautical Information Services)** describe the standardised aeronautical information products, including AIP, amendments, supplements, AIC, NOTAM and aeronautical charts. These are **source families**, not a claim about any current specific airspace boundary or airport status:

- SLCAA, Civil Aviation Regulations Part 15A (official PDF): https://slcaa.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/UPDATED-SLCAR-PART-15A.pdf
- SLAA, Air Navigation Services / Aeronautical Information Services: https://slaa.gov.sl/our-expertise/air-navigation-service-2
- ICAO Aerodrome Design Manual, Part 4, visual aids and holding-position sign principles, published by the Swiss Federal Office of Civil Aviation: https://www.bazl.admin.ch/dam/bazl/en/dokumente/Fachleute/Flugplaetze/ICAO/icao_doc_9157_aerodromedesignmanual-part4.pdf.download.pdf/icao_doc_9157_aerodromedesignmanual-part4.pdf

The common runway naming principle relates the runway designator to its **approximate rounded magnetic direction**. The teaching calculator demonstrates reciprocals but does not calculate local magnetic variation, parallel runway L/C/R suffixes, official runway changes or any designator for a real aerodrome. A diagram displaying a runway holding position teaches awareness, **not a taxi, take-off, landing or runway-entry clearance**.

This content is **not** a verified statement that a specific Sierra Leone AIP, NOTAM, local airspace class, control zone, ICAO operating condition or runway is current. Before an operational lesson or any regulatory claim, an appropriately qualified instructor must check the latest effective national material, actual State source providers, and any applicable local/current publication.
