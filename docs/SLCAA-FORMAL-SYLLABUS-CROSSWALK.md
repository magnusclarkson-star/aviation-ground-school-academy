# Formal syllabus crosswalk — curriculum validation working register
**Status: NOT VALIDATED OR SLCAA-APPROVED** · 13 November 2026

This register maps the Academy's existing 12 public introductory subject modules to **candidate** licensing-theory study topics. It is not an SLCAA-approved PPL(A), PPL(H), CPL, ATPL, instrument rating or instructor qualification course. The public coursebook's approximate reading minutes are not approved hours and cannot satisfy a minimum-hours requirement.

SLCAA Part 1B §3.2.2(c) expects the proposed course to document facilities, equipment, FSTD where applicable, aerodromes and aircraft for flight training, instructor qualifications and a syllabus including prerequisites, lesson objectives, standards, planned lesson time, expected outcomes, stage benchmarks and tests. Part 1A provides the personnel licensing and category-specific requirements. The current editions and relevant implementing standards must be checked by qualified aviation instructors and the Authority.

## Release controls

1. A qualified instructor and course designer produces the complete syllabus revision, stage and lesson standards, assessment strategy and references.
2. An independent quality manager reviews the evidence and requires corrections to any unsupported claims.
3. The accountable manager submits the correct documents to the Authority and receives written approval for the specified course/scope.
4. Only then may a secure Academy course record be labelled as a regulator-approved **course**; passing its tests cannot confer a pilot licence.
5. A material syllabus amendment must be processed and authorised through the applicable SLCAA change-control procedures.

**No individual SLCAA inspector or instructor has signed this crosswalk.**
