## Classroom coded report examples

The coded reports use the **invented** aerodrome station `ZZZZ`. They are never fetched from a weather service and cannot describe the current weather at a real airport. Clicking a group displays an explanation; no report is downloaded, stored or interpreted as live information.

**Fictional METAR:**

```text
METAR ZZZZ 141200Z 21012KT 6000 -RA SCT018 BKN035 26/23 Q1010=
```

**Fictional TAF:**

```text
TAF ZZZZ 141100Z 1412/1512 22010KT 9999 SCT025 TEMPO 1414/1418 4000 TSRA BKN015CB=
```

For clarity, 141200Z means **day 14 at 12:00 UTC**, not local time. The fictional METAR's visibility group `6000` is 6,000 metres in this international example. The TAF temporary period `1414/1418` is **day 14, 14:00–18:00 UTC**; the `TSRA` group indicates a thunderstorm with rain. Cloud heights in the coded groups refer to height above aerodrome level. METAR and TAF formats and State practices must be checked against the relevant country and product specifications before operational use.

The comparison includes a short, pictorial cloud-development aid and a two-row forecast timeline, both explicitly conceptual. No procedural aircraft operations, flight decisions or local Sierra Leone weather claims are presented.
