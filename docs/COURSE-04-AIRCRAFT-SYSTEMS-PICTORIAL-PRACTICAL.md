# Course 04 — Powerplants and Aircraft Systems: pictorial practical
**Date:** 14 November 2026 · **Curriculum status:** `SME_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · **Scope:** existing Ground School subject `systems`

The approved Course 04 text, three core lessons, advanced lessons, illustrations, quiz answers and original simulator aircraft are **unchanged**. This is an additive picture-led classroom practical placed below Study Activity on `academy-coursebook.html?module=systems`.

## Four original picture exercises

| Classroom exercise | Exact original-picture view | Educational activity |
|---|---|---|
| Energy and propulsion | `overview-07` | Conceptual fuel/electrical energy, propulsion and differences among piston engines, gas turbines, propellers and helicopter transmissions |
| Fuel distribution | `detail-06` | Three-block functional diagram: storage → type-specific distribution → engine fuel delivery |
| Electrical power | `detail-06` | Interactive toggle comparing two imaginary displays attached to one common bus against separately illustrated supply paths |
| Hydraulic actuation | `photo-04` and `overview-07` | Describe hydraulic energy transfer and factual reporting of a fictional ambiguous gear indication without inferring the cause |

The original approved photographs and diagrams remain unchanged and are displayed as cropped canvas views with tap-to-enlarge. Students answer three-choice questions, receive reasoned feedback, and open terminology panels. Scouts/Air Scouts and enthusiasts/aspiring pilots see appropriate pathway cues.

**Safety boundary:** these examples do not identify real defects, supply real aircraft maintenance instructions, issue airworthiness statements, specify system pressures/limits, tell students to operate aircraft controls or endorse resetting circuits or opening lines. The training pictures may not visually show the fictional faults described in the stories.

## In-app-only learning materials

The app retains the existing course-note print/Save-as-PDF browser guard; it does not add course-note downloads or exports. The new practical stores answers only in current page memory and makes no network requests. Separate authorised certificate and transcript document actions are unaffected.

This is a browser-only restriction rather than full DRM: publicly hosted teaching content can still be screenshotted or copied. True protection requires privately hosted authenticated lessons and content access controls.
