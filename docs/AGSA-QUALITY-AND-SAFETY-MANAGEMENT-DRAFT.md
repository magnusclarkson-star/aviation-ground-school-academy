# Draft Quality and Safety Management Programme — Aviation Ground School Academy
**AGSA-QSMS-001, Version 0.1 — INTERNAL PLANNING ONLY. No SLCAA acceptance or signed staff approvals.**

## Quality governance

The Accountable Manager allocates financial and staff resources. The independent Quality Manager controls the audit schedule and can escalate unresolved nonconformities. The Head of Training manages course delivery and corrective instruction. A Safety Manager is appointed if required by the ATO scope. Names, licence numbers, qualifications and contact details are not recorded in this public document.

### Quality review cycle

1. **Plan** a risk-based annual audit programme covering each authorised course, management personnel and training records. Maintain independence; an auditor should not audit their own work.
2. **Sample** approved lesson versions, attendance, instructor currency, student progression, examination reports and certificate records.
3. **Record** nonconformity reference, applicable rule, evidence location, severity, affected students and immediate containment.
4. **Correct** the cause with a responsible person, target date, training revision and independent verification.
5. **Report** recurring themes, findings and overdue actions to management and, where applicable, the Authority.
6. **Retain** signed audit working papers in a private, access-controlled system, with regulator access where required.

### Safety management (if applicable)

Identify risks from facilities, actual aircraft/flight activities, electronic training media, instruction, fatigue, youth activities and equipment. Record hazards, potential outcomes, risk assessment and mitigation owners. Maintain emergency response and occurrence-reporting plans appropriate to the approved scope. Do not treat a public virtual flight as actual FSTD training credit. Align the documented SMS to Part 19 and SLCAA acceptance.

### Secure records and student welfare

The private portal is restricted to adult accounts pending approved safeguarding measures. Implement guardian consent, identity/age verification, staff vetting, youth-adult separation and incident escalation before any connected Air Scout enrolment. Do not place student names or medical details in public repositories.

Use restricted database roles, encryption at rest and in transit, reviewable MFA, rotating secrets, controlled backups, restore testing, access reviews and incident response. Prescribed retention under Part 1B must be reconciled with Sierra Leone privacy/data protection obligations before automated deletion.
