# Course 03 — Principles of Flight and Aerodynamics: pictorial practical

**Added:** 14 November 2026 · **Academic state:** `SME_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · **Deployment:** public classroom self-study, not accredited or aircraft-specific training

## What the learner sees

Course 03 adds a **picture-led aerodynamics laboratory** in the existing Ground School Coursebook, after the Study Activity. The three core lessons, existing textbook chapters, diagrams, quizzes and approved visual layout are left untouched.

The four optional activities use source-pixel crops from the Academy's **existing owner-approved original artwork**; the full approved artwork, aircraft models and simulator controls are not edited or replaced.

| Activity | Original picture | What the student practises |
|---|---|---|
| Four forces of flight | `overview-02` — original airfoil and relative airflow | Distinguishes lift, weight, thrust and drag; the diagram's arrows are conceptual, not measured values |
| Angle of attack | `detail-02` — original aerodynamic wing-section panel | Defines AOA using wing chord and relative airflow rather than pitch angle alone |

Every picture can be enlarged with touch/click. Each question has three possible answers, a subject-specific explanation, and a hidden-until-open concept key. Scouts/Air Scouts and enthusiasts/aspiring pilots have separate prompts.

## Interactive classroom bank illustration

A slider shows **0°–60° in 5-degree increments**, in an **idealised steady, coordinated, constant-altitude level turn** at unchanged weight and configuration. Its transparent relationships are:

- Load factor \(n = 1 / \cos(\phi)\).
- Relative stall-speed multiplier at the same weight and wing configuration: \(V_{S,\mathrm{relative}} = \sqrt{n}\).
- 0° = 1.00 g, 1.00× relative stall-speed factor.
- 45° ≈ 1.41 g, 1.19×.
- 60° = 2.00 g, 1.41×.

No specific aircraft speed, flight-control setting, operating limit, actual aerodynamic condition, stall recovery or recommended manoeuvre is shown. These idealised relationships **do not include** turbulence, asymmetric flight, structural restrictions, aircraft-specific data or human factors and **must not be used as flight instructions**.
