## 1. Regulatory route and intended scope

The Academy is currently a publicly accessible **independent education prototype**. It must not advertise itself as an SLCAA-certificated approved training organisation (ATO) until the Authority has issued the applicable certificate and training specifications. SLCAR Part 1B §2.3.1 prohibits advertising as certificated before issuance and requires certificate identification in advertisements once authorised. **Do not imply ICAO, SLCAA, FAA or EASA endorsement.**

Proposed initial discussion with SLCAA: whether the independent online theoretical-knowledge school can receive a defined approved syllabus rating, and which organisational approval, personnel, facilities, assessment and oversight requirements apply. **Do not assume a web-only course can qualify as a flight school.** A proposal to provide flight training requires suitable training aircraft, aerodromes, instructors and facilities. The public 3D simulator is an educational demonstration and may not be logged as approved synthetic flight training time absent device qualification and the Authority's specific acceptance.

## 2. Application sequence and sign-off gates

AC-PEL048 describes five phases. The named applicant and the Authority must agree the actual process and acceptability of each item:

| Phase | Applicant evidence | Required human sign-off | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-application | Contact SLCAA, obtain and complete prospective ATO pre-assessment statement PATOPS/O-PEL-026; describe proposed scope | Legal applicant, SLCAA pre-application team | **Not started** |
| Formal application | Appropriate ATO application, draft training specifications, schedule of events, manuals, management CVs and facilities | Accountable manager and SLCAA | **Not submitted** |
| Document evaluation | Training and Procedures Manual, course syllabi, instructor competence, QMS/SMS, records and compliance statement | Qualified instructor/quality manager, SLCAA evaluators | **Drafts incomplete** |
| Demonstration and inspection | Inspectable facilities, systems, qualified personnel, teaching activities and records | SLCAA inspectors | **Not scheduled** |
| Certification | Authority-issued signed ATO certificate with approved training specifications and course scope | **SLCAA only** | **Not issued** |

Part 1B §2.3.2(b) sets a general application lead time of at least **120 calendar days before proposed training** and **90 days for amendments**, absent Authority-approved shorter periods; separate §3.2.2(a)(2) refers to applying for course approval at least 30 days before planned course training. Ask SLCAA to confirm how the timing provisions apply to the proposed scope. Never interpret these as automatic approval dates.

## 4. Distinguish four approvals

1. **Organisation ATO certificate:** a signed Authority approval for the named legal entity, place and validity.
2. **Training specifications and course authority:** approved scope, courses, facilities, limitations and instructor/equipment authorisations. A certificate does not automatically cover all aviation training.
3. **Instructor and examiner privileges:** specific licence/ratings, currency and any SLCAA examining authority. Merely naming someone a platform instructor does not grant aviation qualifications.
4. **Graduation/course completion:** a documented, properly supervised result within the authorised course. The Academy cannot issue a pilot licence or regulator certificate. An approved-course completion record is distinct from an SLCAA licensing decision.

The secure operations backend checks for separate externally issued documentary evidence and independent internal QA review. That review is NOT a real-time SLCAA approval search.

## 5. Student assessment and records control

SLCAR Part 1B §2.3.12 specifies a published minimum **two-year** retention after completion for detailed student records and after staff cessation for instructor/examiner records; confirm the whole applicable regime before defining retention/deletion workflows. Preserve lesson attendance, progress, stage achievements, exam version and outcome, instructor/examiner identity, student eligibility, remedial action and applicable approvals. Include periodic QA sampling, appeals and tamper-resistant audit history.

Public browser self-check scores cannot be imported into official examination records as proof of knowledge. Regulated assessments require an approved course, authorised examiner, controlled private item bank or externally administered evidence, proper student identity checks, retake rules and an approved documented testing process.
