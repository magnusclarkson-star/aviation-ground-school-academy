Realistic Six-Pack Instrument Studio

Explore the traditional six primary flight instruments. Change the flight conditions and watch the gauges respond. Use the optional audible instructor to learn each instrument at your own pace.

Flight condition controls

These instruments are animated educational approximations. Airspeed and altitude are independently controlled; vertical speed is independently simulated. Turn coordination is simplified and the heading indicator is not a magnetic compass. Not for operational flight training or aircraft use.

Guided learning challenge

Set a heading of 090°, airspeed 100 knots, altitude 5,000 feet, and level wings.
