What we must build before seeking approval

1 · Curriculum and instructors

Approved-scope course outlines, explicit learning objectives, reference editions, author/reviewer sign-off, instructor qualifications, and instructor-facing learning progress.

2 · Assessments and student evidence

Secure student accounts, attendance, examination integrity, versioned results, skill reviews, audit trails and suitable privacy/retention controls. Browser localStorage cannot be a regulator-grade record.

3 · Simulator validation

Aircraft-specific POH/AFM source data, control-response verification, instrument calibrations, testing evidence and device qualification if ever pursuing training-hour credit.

4 · Organisation and quality

Training procedures, safety/quality management, competency checks, formal authority correspondence and controlled updates to manuals.

5 · Offline and accessibility

Low-bandwidth lessons, narrated accessibility, phone and desktop testing, secure syncing, and a protected learning environment for younger Air Scouts.

6 · Partnership and jurisdiction

SLCAA first for local organisation status; pursue separate agreements with appropriately approved organisations where international licence-related learning is desired.

Practice report review

Our review workspace can inspect unsigned simulated practice exports and prepare an unsanctioned coaching draft. Imports remain in browser memory only, and the draft is never presented as an instructor signature or school assessment.

Architecture only

Protected student accounts

A future identity service, role-based access to assigned learner records, authenticated instructors, audit trail and private evidence storage are required before real learner records can be collected. Read the security blueprint.

Awaiting evidence

Approval and verified sign-off

Formal assessments, official learning records, training credit or certificates remain disabled until a suitably authorised training organisation and competent authority permit their actual use.

Publication and evidence controls

  1. Every course and cockpit model should identify its author, reviewer, source documents and revision history.
  2. No certificate or pilot logbook credit is issued based on browser interactions alone.
  3. Use the public readiness register for non-sensitive workflow status. Sensitive student records, regulatory correspondence and keys must remain in a secure, access-controlled backend.
  4. An actual authority's written approval, permitted course scope and verification must exist before any public approval claim is made.
