Professional training is more than an online lesson

For aircraft maintenance, EASA Part-66 B2 includes specific licensing knowledge and experience requirements; an Academy electronics module alone does not qualify a licensed aircraft maintenance engineer. Aircraft accident investigation requires appropriate State authority and supervised experience, with prevention rather than blame as its safety objective.

The Academy will develop its own original educational content and may subsequently seek relevant approvals or partnerships. Certificates of independent study must remain clearly distinct from any Authority-approved course records or professional licences.
