03 · PRACTISE THE SEQUENCE

Three flight-learning scenarios

01 · Runway takeoff

Explore the relationship between a fictional throttle, airspeed, pitch and rotation. Continue climbing or experiment with turns once airborne.

02 · Final approach and landing

Use the setup button to place the imaginary aeroplane on a short final. Align with the runway, manage descent and practise a gentle touchdown and rollout.

03 · Rotorcraft lift and landing

Select helicopter cyclic, then use collective to lift, cyclic to translate and the rudder pedals to turn. Practise a gentle return to the marked helipad.

These exercises use a deliberately simplified fictional flight model. The visual scenery and controls resemble aviation hardware but are not accurate aircraft type reproductions. Real helicopter flight requires specialised instruction. Phone vibration is optional, brief and dependent on browser/device support; it is not genuine force feedback.
