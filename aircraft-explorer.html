Important aviation distinction

Control surface deflection produces aerodynamic moments; it does not instantly set aircraft attitude. This teaching model uses an illustrative response, so the displayed bank, pitch and heading are not a physically integrated flight trajectory. A real flight simulator would calculate forces, rates, attitude and instrument lag over time. Flap movement is shown as a mechanical action, not a guaranteed altitude or airspeed change.
