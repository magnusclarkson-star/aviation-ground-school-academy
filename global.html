Community safeguarding and privacy

  • Scout accounts require age-appropriate controls and guardian permission where applicable.
  • Scout content stays in moderated, approved groups by default; worldwide featuring requires additional explicit consent.
  • Disable unsolicited adult-to-minor private messages and public precise-location sharing.
  • Strip photo location metadata on the server, validate file types, scan uploads and offer reporting and removal tools.
  • Use approved public aviation safety resources only; prohibit restricted airport, military and investigation material.
  • Clearly label sponsorships, avoid targeted advertising to minors and suppress disruptive ads during study and examinations.
  • Provide accessible account deletion, retention policies and privacy notices appropriate to each operating jurisdiction.
