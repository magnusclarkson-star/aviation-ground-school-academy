# F/A-18E Super Hornet — exterior 360° reference model

The aviation academy's F/A-18E **Exterior 360°** inspection panel displays a
third-party detailed 3D model hosted by Sketchfab rather than the academy's
older procedural fighter geometry.

- Artist: **KOG_THORNS** (Sketchfab account @ioai25312)
- Model: **Boeing F/A-18E "Super Hornet"**
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/boeing-fa-18e-super-hornet-9e852037bf2141dcb3fda17013958131
- Embed: https://sketchfab.com/models/9e852037bf2141dcb3fda17013958131/embed
- Attribution license shown by publisher: **Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)**
- Modifications: The linked artwork is displayed unchanged by the author-hosted
  embed, and is not repackaged into the app. It is shown beside a credit link.

## Scope and limitations

The external model is enabled **only** for the F/A-18E Exterior 360° viewer.
It loads on demand and requires internet connectivity to Sketchfab. Rendering
and navigation within that iframe are supplied by Sketchfab; the academy's
normal zoom/auto-rotate controls are intentionally disabled for this view.

**The live flight simulator and the separate Interior training view still use
academy-authored procedural meshes and are not replaced by this embed.** For
an offline, animated, unified model, obtain an authorized download of this or
another Super Hornet asset with geometry, textures and licensing information;
convert and optimize it into the native local-mesh rendering pipeline.
