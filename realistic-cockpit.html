Learning guide

Yoke: turning the control wheel commands opposite aileron deflections. Pulling or pushing commands elevator deflection. Rudder pedals: command yaw. Throttle: controls engine power, affecting acceleration and speed. Instruments display the resulting simulated flight state, not direct finger input.

Training simplification: this is a control-response demonstration, not an aerodynamic flight model, certified trainer, or exact replica of a specific aircraft. The aircraft photo is illustrative; the moving aircraft is a separate schematic overlay.
